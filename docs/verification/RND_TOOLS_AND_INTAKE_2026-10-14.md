# RND tools and requested health details — October 14, 2026

## Accepted behavior

- Onboarding follows conditions → food safety → shopping day. Retired condition-details, allergy-details and clinical-evidence URLs redirect. Declarations create no clinical responses or initial Health details forms. First detail writes require a current RND request matching the area and saved profile scope. Historical answers retain their revision checks; clinical profile approval and resolved clarifications remain separate gates.
- Pending swaps are RND-only. Member swap actionability/clearance rules remain. RND search can propose a verified base awaiting the member-case decision, or generation's ranked stored recipe when no library candidate fits. A source swap creates a separate PENDING_REVIEW plan, supersedes the original, retains the RND claim, stales groceries and records rationale/source/serving/filters in audit. It consumes no member swap allowance.
- Canvas tools use icons, accessible labels and hover titles. Move V / Hand H share a dynamic dropdown. Case approval has Swap, Approve, Reject and Add page; base verification has Approve/Reject. Decisions and message drafts become canvas papers. Cancel removes an unsubmitted decision paper without recording a decision. Published questions remain information papers. New approval rationale papers replay read-only with saved layout.
- Natural-language filtering sends only the RND's expression to the existing Gemini JSON service, with shared account/provider limits and the routine lightweight model sequence. Validated numeric constraints support unit conversion and strict inequalities; unsupported/ambiguous clauses fail instead of being silently dropped. Applied limits are visible and revalidated against the complete serving when swapping. Unknowns are excluded. The later nutrition comparison fix below carries available published extended nutrients through source projections; missing source sugar/sodium is never inferred by AI or treated as zero.
